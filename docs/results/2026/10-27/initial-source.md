Independent source reviews found and corrected three integration defects: new recording paths incorrectly required existing media; a screen failure could be ignored while awaiting the camera under the starting phase; and local preview seeks omitted nonzero video track origins. The implementation now separates read/new output resolvers, invalidates a failed starting operation before it can become recording, and applies actual video origins in preview as export already does. Library and exporter consistently reject symlink ancestors. Stop is available during pending start, and quit/edit guards include both pending phases and unacknowledged OS resources. Late delegate acknowledgements remain recoverable after waiter timeout/cancellation. Actual media-clock correspondence remains open below.
